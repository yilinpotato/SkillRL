# Goal: Put two alarmclock into desk.

## Find the two alarmclocks
- First, look at the room description to see which receptacles (desks, sidetables, shelves, drawers, etc.) contain alarmclocks.
- The alarmclocks may be on open surfaces (e.g., sidetable) or inside closed containers (e.g., drawers). If a container is closed, open it and check its contents.
- If you see exactly two alarmclocks somewhere, that is your source. If you see only one, the second may be elsewhere — keep scanning.

## Move each alarmclock into the desk
- Once you locate an alarmclock, go to its location and take it (e.g., `take alarmclock 1 from sidetable 1`).
- If you are already holding an alarmclock, go directly to the desk and put it in (e.g., `move alarmclock 1 to desk 1`).
- Repeat for the second alarmclock.
- Note: you can also first collect both alarmclocks in your inventory (by taking both) and then go to the desk and move them one by one.

## When to stop
- After you have placed both alarmclocks in the desk, the task is complete. You may stop as soon as you see a success message or when your inventory is empty and both alarmclocks are on the desk.
